### 3. Key Features of Official Datasets

**LogiQA:**
- 8,678 logical reasoning questions
- Covers: categorical, disjunctive, conditional reasoning
- Source: https://github.com/lgw863/LogiQA-dataset

**RuleTaker:**
- Explicit rule-based reasoning
- Supports deep chain reasoning (≥10 steps)
- Uses Problog-generated labels
- Source: S3 Bucket

**ProofWriter:**
- Generates entailment relations, proofs, abductive statements
- Outputs proof depth (0-step/1-step/>1-step/contradiction)
- Source: DOI:10.57702/rexidrxv

**Humanity's Last Exam:**
- 2,700 expert-level closed-book questions
- Multi-modal (text)
- Current SOTA: <10% accuracy ⚠️
- Source: HuggingFace: cais/hle

**ARC-AGI-3:**
- 150+ game-like environments
- 1000+ difficulty levels
- Requires toolkit download
- Source: https://github.com/fchollet/ARC-AGI-3

**CritPt:**
- 71 original physics research questions
- Covers: condensed matter, quantum physics, astrophysics, etc.
- 12 domains
- Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.26574
